Legal / collections terms on this unit

Letter Of Demand
A formal written demand telling the debtor to pay. If the debtor does not respond or pay within the stated period, the matter usually moves toward summons.
Timing: Use the deadline written in the demand itself. The note trail does not prove one universal waiting period, so the dashboard should not invent one. · Source: BLV Law debt collections overview
AOD
An acknowledgement of debt: the debtor admits liability and offers a payment arrangement in writing.
Timing: If the AOD is broken, section 57 mechanics may allow judgment to be sought on the admitted debt path. The key management question is whether default triggered a real legal move. · Source: Magistrates’ Courts Act section 57 summary
